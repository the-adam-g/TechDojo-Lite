## Scope

261 original questions: 161 MCQs, 96 short responses and 4 extended responses. All 53 assessable content subsections are represented; this is a revision bank, not an exhaustive question for every specification bullet or an official assessment. All 12 required practicals have two questions each. The mathematical/data section has 30 questions. Four original synoptic essay titles are available in the unscored planner.

Every question has specification references. Content topics record matching pages in the supplied notes (PDF page numbers, counted from 1). Where notes contain an error or shorthand, the specification takes precedence. No recall of specific codon-to-amino-acid assignments, named bacterial species in nutrient cycles, or production details of monoclonal antibodies is required by this bank.

## How the supplied papers informed the bank

The supplied ZIP contains 23 question papers and 23 paired mark schemes from June 2017–2025 and November 2020–2021, with incomplete early-year coverage. All PDFs were text-extracted for review. Detailed design checks used the June 2025 papers and schemes, especially:

- Paper 1 Q02.4: enzyme model evaluation informed the distinction between a model and a biological explanation.
- Paper 1 Q03: molecular comparisons informed structure/function retrieval questions.
- Paper 2 Q01.1–01.3: glycolysis, carbon dioxide release and electron acceptance informed respiration sequencing and the oxygen/ATP extended question.
- Paper 3 Q02.1–02.3: nephron function and interpretation of animal-study evidence informed kidney questions and an original evaluation scenario with changed sample size and probability.
- Paper 3 Q07 and essay levels on PDF pages 21–25: synoptic links, detailed A-level explanation, accuracy and relevance informed the unscored essay checklist.

Questions, answer choices and model answers are original. They are not transcribed official questions or mark schemes. Exam inspiration is recorded on the two directly modelled extended question formats. Source PDFs are not redistributed in the site ZIP; the official specification link is provided in the guide.

## Marking limits

The existing engine matches keyword alternatives and permits manual ticks. It cannot interpret every valid answer, reject every contradiction or provide an official AQA grade. All Biology written questions carry point-based marking metadata; OCR response levels are not displayed for them. The 25-mark essay is never auto-scored. The four six-mark questions are ordinary extended practice, not complete AQA essay substitutes.

Official paper durations, marks and weighting are shown in the course guide. Custom practice papers do not enforce the official full-paper structure or AO/mathematical/practical percentages. Paper routes filter by specification section, include relevant practicals and shared mathematics. Paper 3 includes the whole bank. Practical endorsement still requires the assessed hands-on activities.

## Notes corrections applied

- Glycolysis is cytoplasmic, not mitochondrial.
- Insects are multicellular; gas transport is through their tracheal system.
- Oxidation is electron loss; photoionisation releases electrons from chlorophyll.
- Osmosis concerns water potential and a partially permeable membrane.
- Statistical significance is not proof of causation or the probability that a hypothesis is true.
- Model answers use the specification rather than unsupported quantitative details in revision notes.
