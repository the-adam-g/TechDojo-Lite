Paper 3: build a synoptic essay

The official essay is worth 25 marks and requires one answer from two titles. It is assessed holistically. This planner helps you practise accurate explanations and connections across at least four topic areas. It does not award a mark or XP. The prompts below are original and stay within the specification.

Review your answer

  • Have you explained relevant A-level biology from at least four distinct topic areas?
  • Does each example explain its importance to the exact title, with links between examples?
  • Are the mechanisms accurate, detailed and expressed with appropriate terminology?
  • Have you removed significant errors and irrelevant material?
  • Ask a teacher to assess the whole response using the official essay levels in the relevant mark scheme. A list of keywords cannot determine an essay mark.

The supplied June 2025 Paper 3 mark scheme, pages 21–25, informed this review approach. This course retains the requested specification boundary; it does not add beyond-specification teaching.

Using this course

MCQs give immediate answers and explanations. Short and extended answers provide indicative points and model answers, with approximate keyword feedback and editable self-assessment. Check the biological meaning of your answer: contradictory or negated statements can still trigger keyword matches. Session scores are revision guidance, not verified AQA grades. Lite has no XP or saved progress.

Practical questions prepare you for written assessment. Your school or college must still arrange and assess the required hands-on practical work and endorsement. Custom papers use the available bank and do not enforce AQA assessment-objective, practical or mathematical weightings.
